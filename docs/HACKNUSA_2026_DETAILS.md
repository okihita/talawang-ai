## 📌 Overview

**HackNusa 2026** is a premier national cybersecurity hackathon organized through a strategic partnership between **Telkom University** and **Kaspersky**. The competition aims to empower young innovators across Indonesia to design, build, and pitch cutting-edge technological solutions addressing modern cybersecurity challenges, digital trust, and emerging AI threats.

---

## 🎯 Competition Tracks

Participants must select one of the following three tracks for their submission:

### 1. Secure Digital Payments & Fintech
* **Focus:** Strengthening security in digital financial ecosystems, payment gateways, and banking services.
* **Key Areas:** Fraud detection and prevention, transaction integrity, authentication protocols, blockchain/distributed ledger security, and payment compliance.

### 2. AI vs AI: Cyber Defense
* **Focus:** Harnessing artificial intelligence and machine learning to counteract sophisticated, AI-driven cyber threats.
* **Key Areas:** Deepfake detection, automated vulnerability scanning, LLM security / prompt injection defense, adversarial ML defenses, and predictive threat intelligence.

### 3. Human-Centric Security
* **Focus:** Addressing the human element in cybersecurity through intuitive UX, behavioral insights, and education.
* **Key Areas:** Human error reduction, phishing/social engineering resilience, gamified cybersecurity training, accessible privacy controls, and security hygiene tools.

## 👥 Eligibility & Team Requirements

* **Target Participants:** Active students (Undergraduate/Diploma) from recognized academic institutions (Universities, Institutes, Polytechnics) across Indonesia.
* **Age Limit:** 16 – 25 years old.
* **Team Size:** 2 – 3 members per team (from the same academic institution).
* **Entry Fee:** **Free of Charge (Gratis)**.
* **Submission Limit:** 1 project per team in 1 selected track.
* **Language:** Submissions and pitch materials are expected in **English**.

## 📦 Submission Requirements (Proof of Concept - PoC)

1. **Project Pitch Deck / Document:**
   - Problem Statement & Background Analysis.
   - Proposed Solution & Technical Architecture.
   - Innovation, Uniqueness, and Competitive Advantage.
   - Technical Feasibility & Implementation Plan.
   - Potential Impact, Scalability, and Viability.
2. **Video Pitch / Demonstration:**
   - Duration: **2 – 3 minutes**.
   - Hosted on YouTube / Vimeo / Google Drive with public/unlisted access.
   - Clear explanation of problem, prototype workflow, and live PoC demo.
3. **Repository / Prototype (if applicable):**
   - Source code link (GitHub/GitLab) or working prototype demo link.
